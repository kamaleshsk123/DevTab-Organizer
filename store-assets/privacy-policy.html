🗂️ DevTab Organizer
Privacy Policy
Last Updated: June 2026

TL;DR: DevTab Organizer collects zero personal data. Everything stays on your device. We have no servers, no analytics, and make no external network requests.

1. Who We Are

DevTab Organizer is a free, open-source Chrome Extension built for developers to organize their browser tabs. The source code is publicly available at github.com/kamaleshsk123/DevTab-Organizer.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of the following:

  • Your browsing history or visited URLs
  • Tab titles or page content
  • Personally identifiable information (name, email, IP address)
  • Usage analytics or telemetry
  • Crash reports or diagnostic data

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

DevTab Organizer stores the following data exclusively on your local device using the Chrome storage.local API:

  • Saved Sessions — Names, tab URLs, and group configurations you explicitly save.
  • Custom Domain Rules — Domain-to-category mappings you define in the settings panel.
  • Extension Settings — Your Auto-Group toggle preference.

This data never leaves your browser. It is not synced to any server and is not accessible to us or any third party.

4. Permissions Explained

  • tabs — Required to read tab URLs and titles to categorize them for grouping. We only access tab data when you actively use the extension.
  • tabGroups — Required to create, update, and manage native Chrome Tab Groups.
  • storage — Required to save your sessions, settings, and custom rules locally on your device.

5. Third-Party Services

DevTab Organizer makes no external network requests. We do not use any third-party SDKs, analytics platforms, or advertising networks. The extension loads Google Fonts (Outfit) in the popup UI, which is a standard browser resource request subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Children's Privacy

Our extension does not collect any data from any users, including children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last Updated" date above and publish the new policy to this page and the GitHub repository.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
